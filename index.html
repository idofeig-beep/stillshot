STILLSHOT

Time moves only when you move.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD
Walk. Time only moves while you move.
MOUSE
Aim (turning moves time a tiny bit)
LEFT CLICK
Shoot / swing  ·  empty hands: grab or punch
RIGHT CLICKQ
Throw what you hold
E
Pick up / swap
HOLD LEFT CLICK
Empty hands, cursor on an enemy: 3 s of lines break him (11 s cooldown)
R
Restart the level
ESCM
Pause  ·  mute
Kill every red enemy, then smash the black pyramid. One hit kills you — and them.
Black things can be picked up. Guns run dry fast: throw them, or take one from a dead enemy.
Bottles, mugs and trays kill when thrown, and block a bullet while you hold them.
Yellow props are tall and stop bullets. White ones are low — bullets fly right over them.

CO-OP

Two players, one room. Time moves whenever either of you moves.
You share 5 hearts: when you both go down, one heart is lost and the room restarts.
Every co-op game is a new run from level 1.
